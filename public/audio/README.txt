This folder is where you can place your personal voice note recording!

File name:
- voice-message.mp3

You can record a voice note on your phone (Voice Memos on iPhone or Voice Recorder on Android), convert or save it as MP3, and place it here!

If no audio file is provided yet, the website comes with a built-in gentle romantic ambient synthesizer so your partner can still press Play and hear an intimate cosmic lullaby.
